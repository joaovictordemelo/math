# Recovering Selmer Invariants from Cyclic Mazur–Tate Leading Terms

**Author:** João Víctor de Mello  
**Date:** November 25, 2026

## Abstract

This paper develops an integral framework for recovering
Selmer invariants of elliptic curves from the leading
augmentation terms of cyclic Mazur–Tate elements.

By combining integral Fitting ideals, Kurihara derivatives,
Euler systems and a factorial-free polarization identity,
we establish, under the stated hypotheses,

$$
\min_K v_p(\operatorname{LT}_r(\Theta_K))
=
v_p(\#\mathrm{Sha}(E)[p^\infty]).
$$

The minimum is interpreted as infinity when the primary
Tate–Shafarevich group is infinite.

## Main Contributions

- Exact minimum-valuation formula for cyclic leading terms.
- Integral polarization without factorial denominators.
- Removal of the restriction $r<p$.
- Recovery of higher Selmer Fitting ideals.
- Explicit computational certificates at 19 primes omitted
  from the Stein–Wuthrich computation.
- A finite arithmetic criterion for primary
  Tate–Shafarevich finiteness.

The principal results are proved under the hypotheses
specified in the manuscript, without assuming the
Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture.

Historical originality remains subject to independent
verification and mathematical peer review.
